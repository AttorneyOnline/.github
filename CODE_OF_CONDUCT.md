## AO Code of Conduct.

Welcome to AO's repositories. Below is our code of conduct, which applies to our repos as well as any communication initiated in the context of these spaces. This code of conduct must be followed by everyone contributing to the AO project, regardless of affiliation or position.

Broadly, interaction should be:

* Civil and respectful.
* About AO or Topic-Relevant Roleplaying aspects.
* About code, not people.

#### **Welcomed:**
- Engaging in discussion over a topic in issues, PRs or development channels.
- Politely and constructively building on each other's ideas with respect to people's time.
- Pointing users to locations or resources for a given topic. So as long as it is done concisely and with proper context.
- Reporting and working through issues in a civil manner with respect to people's time.

#### **Not Welcome:**
- Harassing comments.
- Intimidation.
- Encouraging a person to engage in self-harm.
- Sustained disruption or derailing of threads, channels, lists, etc.
- Offensive or violent comments.
- Unwelcome NSFW or otherwise aggressive attention.
- Continued one-on-one communication after requests to cease.
- Distribution or threat of distribution of people's personally identifying information.
- Presenting any of the above in a light manner unbefitting of it.
  
Failure to follow the code of conduct will be met with punishment based on the severity of the infraction. Ranging from:
- A private or public warning or reprimand.
- A temporary ban from AO and related spaces.
- A permanent ban from AO and related spaces, including loss of contribuiter status.

For any inquiries or reports, please contact the AO Maintainer Team via [Tickets](https://discord.com/channels/278529040497770496/1022028530030231603) in the discord. Or Directly.
